Higher Education Curriculum

Academic Courses Offered

Degree College Upardaha Baraut provides 10 accredited undergraduate, postgraduate, law, teacher education, and vocational programs.

Undergraduate Duration: 3 Years

B.A. – Bachelor of Arts

स्नातक कला संकाय

Hindi Literature, English, Ancient & Modern History, Political Science, Sociology, Sanskrit, and Economics.

Eligibility: 10+2 Any Stream Seats: 480
Undergraduate Duration: 3 Years

B.Sc. – Bachelor of Science

स्नातक विज्ञान संकाय

Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM) and Zoology, Botany, Chemistry (ZBC) with full laboratory practical curricula.

Eligibility: 10+2 with Science Seats: 360
Undergraduate Duration: 3 Years

B.Com. – Bachelor of Commerce

स्नातक वाणिज्य संकाय

Corporate accounting, business mathematics, taxation principles, commercial law, banking, and business economics.

Eligibility: 10+2 Commerce/Science Seats: 180
Postgraduate Duration: 2 Years

M.A. – Master of Arts

परास्नातक कला संकाय

In-depth master degree programs in Hindi Literature, History, Political Science, and Sociology for research and civil exams.

Eligibility: Graduation (B.A./Any) Seats: 120
Postgraduate Duration: 2 Years

M.Sc. – Master of Science

परास्नातक विज्ञान संकाय

Postgraduate study in Chemistry, Mathematics, and Botany equipped with dedicated modern instrumentation.

Eligibility: B.Sc. in relevant subject Seats: 60
Postgraduate Duration: 2 Years

M.Com. – Master of Commerce

परास्नातक वाणिज्य संकाय

Advanced corporate financial reporting, business management strategies, quantitative commerce, and marketing research.

Eligibility: B.Com. Degree Seats: 60
Teacher Education Duration: 2 Years

B.Ed. – Bachelor of Education

शिक्षक प्रशिक्षण संकाय

NCTE recognized pedagogical training, educational technology, school internships, and classroom instruction methodologies.

Eligibility: Graduation with 50% Seats: 100
Physical Education Duration: 2 Years

B.P.Ed. – Bachelor of Physical Ed.

शारीरिक शिक्षा संकाय

Specialized program in sports pedagogy, exercise physiology, athletic conditioning, sports administration, and track coaching.

Eligibility: Graduation + Sports Seats: 50
Law Faculty Duration: 3 Years

LL.B. – Bachelor of Laws

विधि संकाय

Bar Council accredited curriculum covering Constitutional Law, Criminal Laws (BNS/IPC), Civil Code, Jurisprudence, and Moot Courts.

Eligibility: Graduation Any Stream Seats: 120
Technical / Trades Duration: 2 Years

ITI – Industrial Training Institute

व्यावसायिक प्रशिक्षण

Technical vocational training in Electrician and Fitter trades with hands-on heavy workshop machinery and electrical panels.

Eligibility: 10th Standard Pass Seats: 120
